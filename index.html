🚗 Visitor Tips & Etiquette

  • Dim Your Headlights: Please turn off your headlights (use parking lights only) while parked so everyone can enjoy the lights without glare.
  • Be Courteous to Neighbors: Keep music at a respectful volume and avoid blocking Vermont Hill Road or neighboring driveways.
  • Prefer Steady Lights? Not feeling all the flashing lights? Drive by the front of the property to wave at Santa and view a smaller, steady light display.
