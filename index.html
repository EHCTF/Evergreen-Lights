👋 About Us

Welcome! My brother and I officially started synchronizing and building our Christmas light display in 2024 right here at 7586 Vermont Hill Road in Holland, NY.

What started as a fun holiday project between brothers has grown each season with new lights, music, and memories. Every strand, prop, and sequence is set up by hand to bring some extra Christmas cheer to our community.

Thank you for stopping by, sharing the show with friends and family, and making our holiday tradition so special!
