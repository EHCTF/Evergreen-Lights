👋 About Our Family Display

Decorating for Christmas has been a tradition for our family in Holland, NY for over two decades! We have had a big steady holiday light display in front of our house since 2005.

In 2017, the display was greatly expanded by the two oldest children in the family, growing larger and brighter each winter until we officially produced our first full music-synchronized light show in 2024.

Now in our third synchronized season, the show features thousands of lights dancing to over 30 minutes of holiday music alongside our steady display up front where visitors can still wave to Santa. Thank you for making our family's holiday tradition part of yours!
